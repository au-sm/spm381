Part 1 · Foundations

What Is Culture?

“Shared beliefs, attitudes, norms, roles, and values found among speakers of a particular language who live during the same historical period of a specific geographic region.”de Mooij (2004, p. 26)

Cultural Intelligence (CQ)

The capability to relate and work effectively in culturally diverse settings.

How is it built?

Mostly through experience: travel, study abroad, global internships, working on mixed teams.

Why it matters in sport

Leagues, clubs, and brands now sell to fans in dozens of countries at once.

Definition: de Mooij (2004), p. 26. Cultural intelligence (CQ): Earley & Ang (2003); Forbes/IESE (2015) on developing CQ through experience: https://www.forbes.com/sites/iese/2015/03/24/why-you-need-cultural-intelligence-and-how-to-develop-it/

Debate

Are Esports a Sport?

The case for “yes”

  • Medal events at the Asian Games (2022 and 2026)
  • Pro leagues, transfer fees, sponsors, and sold-out arenas
  • Saudi Arabia launches an Esports Nations Cup (Nov 2026)

The case for “not yet”

  • The IOC and Saudi Arabia cancelled the Olympic Esports Games deal in Oct 2025; no new host yet
  • Video game addiction: the WHO lists “gaming disorder” as a health condition
  • Game publishers, not federations, own the rules
DISCUSSAre video games/competitions a sport? What would it take to convince you?
IOC and Saudi Arabia ended the 12-year Olympic Esports Games partnership in Oct 2025; IOC says it will develop a new approach. Saudi Arabia: Esports Nations Cup starting Nov 2026. WHO added "gaming disorder" to ICD-11 (adopted 2019, in effect 2022).

New York → Beijing

Why China? Motivation and Strategy

Motivation

  • The U.S. market was near saturation by the 1990s
  • Basketball is as popular as soccer in China, about 1.4 billion potential fans
  • Bigger markets bring economies of scale in media and merchandise
  • Star bridges: Yao Ming (2002–2011), and now Yang Hansen (Portland, 2025 draft)
  • Streaming lets every game reach phones in China live

Strategy (2026)

  • Glocalization: Chinese New Year games, Chinese-language content and halftime elements
  • Digital partner Tencent: about $1.5B over 5 years (2019 extension, through 2027)
  • Back on state TV (CCTV) since 2022
  • Live games again: NBA China Games in Macao, two a year through 2029
  • Star player summer tours and social media (Douyin, Weibo)
Tencent: 2019 extension reported at about $1.5B over five years (about $300M/yr), reported to run through 2027. Yang Hansen: drafted No. 16 by Portland in 2025. NBA China Games: Sands China agreement, two games a year in Macao through 2029.

New York · Washington

Legal Issues: New York and the Big Stage

  • New York was the last U.S. state to legalize pro MMA (2016)
  • UFC 205 at Madison Square Garden (2016): 20,427 fans, $17.7M gate, still the MSG record
  • UFC 322 at MSG (Nov 2025): 20,664 fans, about $13.6M gate
  • UFC Freedom 250 at the White House (June 14, 2026): about 4,300 fans on the South Lawn
DISCUSSFrom banned in New York to fighting at the White House in ten years: what changed?
New York legalized professional MMA in 2016 (NY State Athletic Commission). UFC 205, MSG, Nov 12, 2016: 20,427 attendance, about $17.7M gate, about 1.3M PPV buys. UFC 322, MSG, Nov 15, 2025: 20,664 attendance, about $13.6M gate (third-highest at MSG). UFC Freedom 250 at the White House, June 14, 2026: about 4,300 attendance, about $60M production cost.

$1.50B

Le v. Zuffa antitrust settlement (2025)
  • No collective bargaining agreement (CBA); fighters sign individual multi-fight contracts as independent contractors
  • Plaintiffs say fighters receive under 20% of UFC revenue, while NBA, NFL, and NHL players get 49–51%
  • A second case, Johnson v. Zuffa, is still in court in 2026

Le v. Zuffa: $375M settlement, final approval Feb 6, 2025. Johnson v. Zuffa (fights from July 2017 on) still pending in D. Nev.; spoliation hearing Feb 2026. UFC 2025 revenue $1.502B, adjusted EBITDA $851M (TKO Group 2025 results). Revenue-share comparison (NBA/NFL/NHL about 49-51%) and under-20% fighter share are as alleged by plaintiffs.
